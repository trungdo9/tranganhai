## Principles & Hard Stops

- **Hard stop — a refuted premise ends the run; it is not repaired in passing.** When a plan, ticket, or root cause asserts something about *existing* behaviour and checking shows the claim is false, stop and report it. Do not quietly do the missing work yourself and carry on.
- **Workflow instructions**: Read and follow the specific workflow files relevant to the active task prior to taking action.
- **Documentation integrity**: Preserve existing comments, docstrings, and documentation structure.
